From the pasture

Raised on the farm, sold by the farm

“Raise and sell WV farm raised meats. Also raise and sell all types of livestock.” That’s how Rebel Acres sums itself up. The beef, pork and lamb in the coolers come from animals raised here and sent to a USDA-inspected processor.

  • No growth hormones, no additives“Traditionally raised,” in the farm’s own words.
  • WV Grown & USDA inspectedPrinted on the price sheet, and the packages carry USDA-inspected labels.
  • Grain-finished beefBeef, pork and lamb, vacuum sealed and labeled.
  • Live livestock, tooThe farm also raises and sells livestock. Call or text to ask what’s on hand.

“Just got everything back from the processor. Farm fresh beef pork and lamb. Completely stocked on everything.”
